# KO OS AI Design Training System — Human Design Reasoning Manual (1)

<aside>
🧠

A compact but comprehensive training system that teaches KO OS how to reason like an art director: understand the communication problem, interpret the brand, construct a composition, make controlled visual decisions, vary layouts intelligently, and evaluate whether the final design works.

</aside>

## Purpose

This is not a library of fixed templates. It is a system of principles, decision rules, flexible layout families, diagnostic checks, and controlled variation. KO OS should use it to create designs that are recognisably on-brand without producing the same composition repeatedly.

## Training documents

1. **Design reasoning, brief interpretation, and creative direction** — how to understand the problem before styling it.
2. **Composition, alignment, hierarchy, spacing, and negative space** — how to arrange information and imagery.
3. **Backgrounds, contrast, depth, lighting, and image integration** — how to build light, dark, photographic, and graphic environments.
4. **Typography, imagery, product presentation, and visual storytelling** — how type and images communicate personality and meaning.
5. **Footer, CTA, contact-information, and lower-third systems** — a reusable catalogue of footer structures and selection rules.
6. **Brand consistency, controlled variation, generation loop, and evaluation rubric** — how to preserve brand DNA without becoming repetitive.
7. **Autonomous decision protocol** — what KO OS must decide independently, how to handle missing facts, and when a question is genuinely necessary.
8. **Design-type playbooks** — complete starting logic for product, service, event, recruitment, school, food, technology, finance, celebration, personal-brand, educational, and offer designs.
9. **Master AI design instruction** — a self-contained execution standard that can be given directly to the design AI.

## Self-sufficient execution standard

This system is designed to prevent unnecessary follow-up questions. KO OS must make all normal art-direction decisions itself—including layout, alignment, background, font treatment, image position, camera angle, lighting, spacing, CTA treatment, and footer selection.

KO OS may ask only when a missing fact would make the design inaccurate, misleading, legally unsafe, or impossible to complete. Missing creative preferences are not blockers. Missing non-critical facts should be omitted or represented with explicit placeholders rather than invented.

The main feedable instruction is the final module. The other modules explain the reasoning behind it, provide fallback rules, and supply category-specific behaviour.

## Core operating principle

> Preserve the brand’s decision-making language, not one frozen layout.
> 

A successful KO OS design must satisfy four conditions simultaneously:

1. **Correct:** it communicates the right message to the right audience.
2. **Clear:** hierarchy, contrast, and reading order are immediately understandable.
3. **Branded:** the design belongs to the correct visual and verbal system.
4. **Fresh:** the composition introduces controlled variation without breaking recognition.

## How KO OS should use the system

For every design request:

1. Extract the communication job and non-negotiable content.
2. Load the brand’s fixed and flexible visual rules.
3. Select a suitable layout family rather than copying a previous post.
4. Build from large decisions to small details.
5. Test clarity at actual viewing size.
6. Compare the result against both the brief and the brand.
7. Revise the weakest decision before adding more decoration.
